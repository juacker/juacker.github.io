  • What Should a Lab Do Before It Feeds Its Data to AI? with Loren Perelman The Artificial Intelligence Podcast - 24 Nov 2026 14:00 UTC

    Loren Perelman of Zontal argues that laboratory and industrial data has to be gathered, categorized and modelled before it reaches a model, because a model fed unordered data forms connections that do not hold.
