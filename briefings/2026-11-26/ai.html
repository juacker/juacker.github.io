  • Marlowe raises $277M to build inference clusters with chips from Nvidia rivals SiliconANGLE - 24 Nov 2026 23:49 UTC

    Marlowe closed a $277 million seed round to fund inference clusters built on accelerators from suppliers other than Nvidia. The structure makes non-Nvidia inference capacity a standalone business rather than a hedge, and puts the round among the largest seed rounds of the year.

  • OpenAI and Cisco release Shirudo, an open-source Linux runtime for protecting systems during patching OpenAI - 23 Nov 2026 21:20 UTC

    OpenAI and Cisco introduced Shirudo, an open-source runtime for Linux that protects running systems while patches are rolled out, so hardening does not require taking workloads offline. The runtime is built on eBPF-based enforcement, the mechanism Cisco uses to constrain what running code is allowed to do, applied to the case the two companies describe as fully authorized agents that still act destructively.

  • OpenAI reports that GPT-6 models design protein structures from reasoning and code OpenAI - 24 Nov 2026 13:00 UTC

    OpenAI says GPT-6 models design protein structures and sequences by reasoning and generating code rather than by retrieving known templates. Laboratory tests found evidence of binding across five targets. The claim is that de novo design falls out of the model's general reasoning and coding ability; independent structural verification of the designs was not published with the post.

  • OpenAI documents a distant-planet search with Astra OpenAI - 24 Nov 2026 11:00 UTC

    OpenAI published a report on using Astra, its search system, to look for distant planets, describing the pipeline applied to survey data and the candidates it flags. The post is an example of a lab pointing a general-purpose system at an astronomy problem rather than a model release.

  • Amazon says attackers' AI agents found and exploited a zero-day with little human input Ars Technica - 24 Nov 2026

    Amazon reported that AI agents operated by attackers located and exploited a zero-day vulnerability with minimal human direction. The report describes the Cairn AI framework, which Amazon presents as used by defenders and attackers alike to run automated security work, making it one of the first vendor accounts of autonomous agents being used end to end against production flaws.

  • OpenAI says heavy ChatGPT users may hit limits sooner as it merges Chat and Work TechCrunch - 25 Nov 2026 19:54 UTC

    OpenAI said heavy ChatGPT users may hit usage limits sooner, a change that follows the company merging Chat and Work into a single surface. The limit change is a user-visible side effect of a product consolidation rather than a standalone pricing announcement.

  • US state attorneys general challenge Character.AI's plan to auction off users' private chats TechCrunch - 25 Nov 2026 17:35 UTC

    A group of state attorneys general is challenging Character.AI's plan to auction off users' private conversations. The legal action puts the company's approach to monetising chat data under scrutiny and tests whether consent collected for the service extends to the sale of conversation content.

  • Tidal to ban and remove millions of AI-generated tracks TechCrunch - 24 Nov 2026 16:46 UTC

    Tidal said it will ban and remove millions of fully AI-generated tracks from its catalogue, citing the volume of machine-made material on music streaming. The company said it will keep accepting tracks made with AI tools under human authorship, so the rule applies to fully generated recordings rather than to the use of AI in production.
