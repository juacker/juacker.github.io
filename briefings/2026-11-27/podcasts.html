  • How Clay Builds GTM Agents - Jeff Barg, Head of AI at Clay

    Latent Space - 26 Nov 2026 22:44 UTC

    Jeff Barg, head of AI at Clay, describes building go-to-market infrastructure that any agent can use: usage at Clay is split roughly 50/50 between its own agent and external harnesses such as Claude Code. He covers the eval-driven approach behind the company's coding agent Sculptor, the decision to keep running agents on a queue system built with BullMQ rather than Temporal or LangGraph, and why the team sees more value in frontier models for orchestration and deep research than in choosing a model for customers. Clay raised $115 million at a $7.1 billion valuation in September and the company has grown to around 600 people.

  • Can AI Really Speed Up Drug Discovery? - Evan Stoll, Cresset

    The Artificial Intelligence Podcast - 26 Nov 2026 14:00 UTC

    Evan Stoll of Cresset explains the move of laboratory work out of spreadsheets and paper notebooks, where an untested tweak can decide whether a programme succeeds, and why a scientist stays in the loop to validate what the models propose, with experimental checks such as binding assays. He says Cresset's Flare V13 ships an agent that works with the model a scientist already uses, such as Claude or GitHub Copilot, and that work which previously took five or six hours now takes 15 to 20 minutes with the newer models, which lets small teams move faster. He expects note-taking to head towards automatic capture of conversations linked to laboratory databases, though the tools are not there yet.
