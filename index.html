About

I'm a software engineer based in Madrid with 10+ years in backend and distributed systems. Currently at Netdata, where I lead the multi-agent AI system that analyses infrastructure data for users, and work on the backend that monitors millions of nodes. On the side I build CLAI, an open-source orchestrator for teams of AI agents, in Rust.

Previously I worked on observability at BBVA, billing and payments at OVHcloud, and spent a few years trying to build my own company, Komlog, a SaaS platform for system monitoring that extracted metrics from unstructured data.

Telecom engineer by training, with a Master in AI and Deep Learning.
